About Abuti

Abuti's Fast Food was started in 2023 with not just one goal but multiple: to serve proper SA street food with pride. To increase the number of orders by making themselves available online and on Whatsapp. To be able to get people's honest feedback on what they think about their goods and services. To also provide the food they rendor online. We specialize in Bunny Chow but we also do fat cakes, burgers, dagwoods and more. Every meal is made fresh, with love and lots of flavor.

Our Mission

To bring people together over good food that tastes like home. To serve authentic, affordable South African fast food with heart. At Abuti's, we bring people together through good food, friendly service, and a taste of home in every bunny chow.

Our Vision

To be Johannesburg's favorite spot for fast, affordable, authentic meals. To be the leading provider of authentic South African fast food, with multiple branches across the country, while keeping quality and affordability at the center.
