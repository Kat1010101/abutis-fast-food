# Abuti's Fast Food 🍔

Welcome to the official website repository for **Abuti's Fast Food**! Established in 2023, Abuti's Fast Food serves authentic, affordable South African street food made fresh with love and lots of flavor.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This repository contains the static web pages for Abuti's Fast Food website. The website allows customers to browse the menu, learn about the brand, locate branches, and easily place orders via WhatsApp.

### Key Pages
* **`index.html`** – Home page featuring a hero banner, quick intro, and welcome message.
* **`menu.html`** – Full menu displaying available fast food items, descriptions, and pricing.
* **`about.html`** – Background story, mission, and vision of Abuti's Fast Food.
* **`contact.html`** – Contact details and physical branch locations embedded with Google Maps.
* **`order.html`** – Direct WhatsApp ordering portal for fast customer orders.

## 📲 How Ordering Works

1. Visit the **Order** page (`order.html`).
2. Click the **Order on WhatsApp** button.
3. Fill in your name, order details, and preference (Pickup or Delivery).
4. Send the message directly to Abuti to place your order!
